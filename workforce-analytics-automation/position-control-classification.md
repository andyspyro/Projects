# Position Control and Classification Validation

This work focused on keeping position data accurate enough for workforce reporting, hiring, and salary actions.

## Position Review

I reviewed fields such as:

* position status
* vacancy status
* job classification
* reporting group
* filled or vacant state
* recruitment activity
* salary related attributes
* effective dates

## Validation Questions

Typical checks included:

* Is the position actually vacant?
* Is an active recruitment already attached to it?
* Does the classification match the reporting record?
* Does the position appear consistently across systems?
* Is a filled position still being counted as vacant?
* Is a closed or changed position still appearing in an active report?

## Classification Support

Classification information also had to be consistent with salary and recruitment reporting.

When it did not match, I investigated the discrepancy before allowing the record to flow into summary reporting.

## Why it mattered

Position control is a foundation for workforce analytics.

If the underlying position population is wrong, every vacancy rate, fill rate, and staffing trend calculated from it will also be wrong.
