# Onboarding and Hiring Process Workflow

This work covered operational tracking from recruitment through onboarding.

The public version keeps the process generic and removes employer specific stage names.

## Process Areas

The workflow included:

* applicant status
* interview status
* screening or background processing
* reference checks
* salary review
* approval
* offer
* onboarding preparation
* document readiness
* start scheduling
* completion

## Handoffs

A major purpose of the tracker was to make handoffs between teams visible.

A record could be delayed even when no one individual step was very long if it repeatedly waited between owners.

## Onboarding Approval Workflow

I also worked with an approval form workflow that included:

* submitted items
* review
* corrections
* supporting document loading
* final approval
* completion

## Operational Support

The broader workflow work included maintaining application and hiring status information and supporting the systems used during hiring operations.

## Why it mattered

The most important field in an operational workflow is often not the final status. It is the combination of current owner, current stage, time in stage, and next required action.
