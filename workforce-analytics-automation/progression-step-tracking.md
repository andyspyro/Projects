# Progression and Step Tracking

I maintained tracking used to monitor employees moving through scheduled progression or salary step processes.

The public summary removes all employee names, IDs, dates, and position identifiers.

## Tracking Fields

The workflow included concepts such as:

* current progression status
* current step
* expected next step
* eligibility or review timing
* action completed
* action pending
* exception or follow up needed
* archive status

## Purpose

The tracker helped make scheduled actions visible before they became overdue.

It also gave a consistent place to identify records that needed review.

## Data Quality

The progression workflow required checking that:

* the correct record was being tracked
* prior actions had been completed
* step information matched the source system
* expected timing was reasonable
* completed items were removed from the active workload

## Why it mattered

Small operational trackers become important controls when they represent future actions.

The most useful design is one that clearly separates what is complete, what is due next, and what requires human review.
