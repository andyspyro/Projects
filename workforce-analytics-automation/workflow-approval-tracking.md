# Workflow and Approval Tracking

I used Smartsheet and SharePoint based workflows to give HR staff better visibility into actions that moved between multiple people or approval stages.

## Workflow Concepts

The trackers included items such as:

* request submitted
* review pending
* correction needed
* supporting document status
* approval pending
* approved
* returned for revision
* complete
* archived

## Why I Built It

Email alone makes it difficult to answer:

* Who currently owns this action?
* How long has it been waiting?
* Was it returned for correction?
* Is a required document missing?
* Is the action actually complete?

A shared workflow made those questions easier to answer.

## Smartsheet

Smartsheet was useful for:

* structured status tracking
* shared updates
* workflow visibility
* approval tracking
* correction and document follow up
* operational reporting

## SharePoint

SharePoint was used as part of reporting and shared HR information workflows, including vacancy related reporting and team access to current information.

## Why it mattered

Workflow design is mostly about making ownership and state explicit.

If the next person cannot tell what happened before the item reached them, the process will create delays even if the underlying system works correctly.
