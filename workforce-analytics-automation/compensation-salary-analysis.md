# Compensation and Salary Analysis

This work focused on salary actions, internal equity review, step placement validation, and compensation related workflow tracking.

## Analysis Areas

I worked with information such as:

* proposed salary
* approved salary
* salary range
* employee or candidate placement within the range
* step placement
* salary action status
* approval stage
* internal equity context
* effective date
* supporting position information

## Validation

The work required checking whether:

* the salary aligned with the approved range
* the correct step or placement rule was being applied
* the position information matched the salary action
* the approval status was current
* the effective date was consistent
* the same salary value was reflected across reporting sources

## Internal Equity

I also supported analysis that compared salary decisions with relevant internal context.

The public project does not include real salaries or employee information.

## Workflow Tracking

Salary actions were also part of the broader hiring timeline.

Tracking when salary review started and when approval was returned helped separate compensation delays from other hiring delays.

## Why it mattered

Compensation analytics requires both numerical accuracy and process accuracy.

A number can be valid mathematically and still be wrong if it belongs to the wrong position, step, effective date, or approval stage.
