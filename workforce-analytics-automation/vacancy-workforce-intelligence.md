# Vacancy and Workforce Intelligence

This project focused on turning position and staffing data into a workforce view that leadership could use for planning.

## Measures

The reporting included measures such as:

* authorized positions
* filled positions
* vacant positions
* positions in an active hiring process
* vacancy rate
* fill rate
* hiring pipeline rate
* vacancy age
* vacancy aging groups
* workforce trend over time
* hiring activity by reporting group

## Vacancy Aging

A vacancy count alone does not show urgency.

I added aging logic so long running vacancies could be separated from newly opened vacancies.

That made it easier to focus attention on positions that had remained unfilled for extended periods.

## Multi Site Reporting

The reporting model supported multiple operational locations while still allowing a combined workforce view.

The public version removes all real location names.

## Leadership Use

The dashboard was intended to answer:

* Where are the largest staffing gaps?
* Which vacancies have been open the longest?
* Which positions are already moving through recruitment?
* Is the vacancy trend improving or getting worse?
* Which areas need recruitment attention first?

## Why it mattered

The most useful workforce metric is often not a total. It is the combination of status, age, trend, and process stage.
